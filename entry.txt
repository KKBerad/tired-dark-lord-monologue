Behold. The eclipse is aligned, the armies are assembled, and the ancient seal is — one moment, let me sit down. Three hundred years of monologuing from this parapet, and my knees have filed a complaint. The plan remains flawless: at dawn I unmake the kingdom, scatter its banners, and finally — finally — take a nap. I conquered the northern wastes before breakfast and all I got was this drafty cape. So yes, hero, draw your sword. Just give me a minute. The throne can wait. It has waited this long.
